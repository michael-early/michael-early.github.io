Economics / Machine Learning

Economic growth in developed and developing countries

Collaborative empirical project using macroeconomic indicators, HDI data, clustering, and Random Forest regression to compare drivers of GDP growth.

Project Overview

This study analyzes how macroeconomic factors relate to growth across development levels. It combines unsupervised clustering with supervised regression to compare feature importance between developed and developing country groups.

Methods

Clustering

K-means groups countries using development and macroeconomic indicators.

Regression

Random Forest models estimate GDP growth relationships and rank predictors.

Economic interpretation

Results compare investment, education, inflation, and development-level dynamics.
